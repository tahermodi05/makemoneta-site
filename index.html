FAQ

Questions people ask

What is performance marketing?

Performance marketing means running ads where every result is counted: how many people called, messaged or bought, and what each one cost. Instead of paying for ads and hoping, you see exactly which ads bring customers, and the budget goes to those. That is what a performance marketing agency like Moneta does.

What does a Meta ads or Google Ads agency do?

A Meta ads agency runs your ads on Facebook and Instagram. A Google Ads agency runs your ads on Google Search, YouTube and Google Maps. We do both: we decide who sees your ads, make the ads, set the budget, check the results every day and change what isn't working. You get the customers and a simple weekly report.

What is lead generation?

A lead is a person who shows interest in what you sell, for example by calling, sending a WhatsApp message or filling in a form. Lead generation means bringing you more of these people through ads, your website and Google. We focus on leads that turn into paying customers, not just more messages.

How much should I spend on ads?

It depends on your goal and market. Many small businesses start with ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 a month in India, or $500 to $1,500 a month in the US, UK or Australia. We'll recommend a figure in your free audit.

How fast will I see results?

Search ads can bring inquiries in the first week. Meta and LinkedIn usually need 2 to 4 weeks of testing to settle. We tell you upfront what to expect and when.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can guarantee sales from ads. What we guarantee is clear tracking, weekly reporting and that every rupee is spent on purpose.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. Always.

How much does it cost?

Every business needs a different mix of platforms and budget, so we quote after your free audit. You get a written quote before any work starts. Ad spend is always paid by you directly to Google, Meta or LinkedIn, and we never mark it up.

Where are you based, and where do you work?

Moneta is based in India and works with businesses anywhere: India, the US, the UK, Australia, the Middle East and beyond. We work on your time zone.

What do you need from me to start?

Access to your current ad accounts (if any), your website, and 30 minutes to tell us about your customers.
