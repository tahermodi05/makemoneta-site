How we work

You see the work before you pay for it

Most agencies ask for money first and show you something weeks later. We do it the other way round.

  1. A short call or visit

    We look at your current website, Google listing and competitors, and ask what kind of buyer you want more of.

  2. Your demo homepage

    We build a working demo of your new homepage and show it to you privately. No charge, no commitment.

  3. Build and launch

    If you like it, you pay 50% and we finish the full site in 5 to 7 working days, using your real photos and numbers.

  4. Inquiries

    After launch, we can run ads to the site and report every week on the leads it brings in.

Questions

Things people usually ask

Is the demo homepage really free?

Yes. We build it so you can see exactly what you would get. If it isn't right for you, you owe nothing and we don't publish it anywhere.

Who owns the domain and the website?

You do. The domain and hosting are bought in your name and paid by you. After the final payment, the website files are yours too.

We already have a website. Can you redo it?

Yes, most of our work is replacing old or slow sites. We keep your domain, move over what is still useful and rewrite the rest.

What do you need from us?

Your logo, product list, a few real photos of your unit, machines and products, and any certifications. If you don't have good photos, we can arrange a visit to take them.

Do you work outside Rajkot and Jamnagar?

Yes. Website and ads work can be done anywhere in India over WhatsApp and video calls. Video editing clients are mostly in the US, UK and Australia.

How do I pay?

Bank transfer or UPI for Indian clients, with a proper invoice. International clients can pay by bank transfer or an agreed payment platform.
