Refund Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains when Moneta, run by Taher Modi in India, refunds payments. Each project also has a written quote or agreement. If that document says something different, the project document applies.

Free work

Free audits, demos and sample edits cost nothing, so there is nothing to refund.

Website, store and landing page projects

  • The advance is refundable in full if you cancel before we start work.
  • Once work has started, the advance covers work already done and is not refundable.
  • The final payment is due at launch and is not refundable after the project goes live.

Ads management and monthly services

  • Monthly fees are paid in advance and cover work for that month. They are not refunded for a month that has started.
  • You can cancel with written notice. After the first term, cancellation applies from the next billing month.
  • Ad spend is paid by you directly to Google, Meta, LinkedIn or other platforms. Moneta cannot refund ad spend; any platform refunds follow that platform's rules.

Video, UGC and photoshoots

  • Fees for creators, photographers, travel or locations booked on your behalf are not refundable once booked.
  • Edited work includes the revisions stated in your quote.

Founding client offer

The founding client discount applies to the fees stated in your agreement and does not change this refund policy.

How to ask for a refund

Write to hello@makemoneta.com with your invoice number. We reply within 5 working days, and approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 working days.
