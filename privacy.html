Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Moneta ("we", "us") collects when you use makemoneta.com or contact us, why we collect it and what rights you have. Moneta is run by Taher Modi from Rajkot, Gujarat, India. We aim to follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules.

What we collect

This website has no login and no tracking cookies. We only receive personal data when you choose to contact us:

  • If you message us on WhatsApp, including through the forms on this website (which open WhatsApp with your details filled in), we receive your name, phone number and anything you write.
  • If you email us, we receive your email address, name and the content of your email.
  • If you become a client, we also keep business details needed for the work and for invoicing, such as your business name, address and GST number.

Why we use it

We use your data to reply to you, prepare a demo or quote you asked for, deliver the work you hire us for, send invoices and keep records the law requires. We do not sell your data and we do not add you to marketing lists without asking.

Services that process data for us

  • Website hosting: our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security.
  • Fonts: pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) and Google Workspace (Gmail) carry our messages and emails, under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

Inquiry messages are kept for up to 12 months if you don't become a client. Client records and invoices are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it where we are not legally required to keep it. You can also withdraw any consent you gave. Write to hello@makemoneta.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Contact and grievances

For any privacy question or complaint, contact Taher Modi at hello@makemoneta.com or +91 99092 52362.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
