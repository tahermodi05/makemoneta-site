Free landing page audit

Is your landing page losing you customers?

A landing page is the page people see after they click your ad. Answer 10 quick questions and get your score out of 100, with the fixes that matter most. Takes about two minutes.

1. Does the page headline match the promise in your ad?
2. Can a visitor tell what you sell within five seconds?
3. Is there one clear main button at the top, without scrolling?
4. Does the page load in under 3 seconds on a phone?
5. Is there a WhatsApp button or click-to-call?
6. Do you show real proof: reviews, photos, numbers or clients?
7. Is your form short (4 fields or fewer)?
8. Is tracking set up, so you can see which ads bring people who contact you?
9. Do you answer the top buyer questions (price, delivery, guarantee)?
10. Does someone reply to new inquiries within an hour?
